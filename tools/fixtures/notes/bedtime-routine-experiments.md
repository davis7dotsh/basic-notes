# Bedtime routine experiments

goal: in bed by 11, actually out by 11:30 instead of doom scrolling till 1am. running each change ~2 wks so i can tell what's doing what.

## the list
- [x] magnesium glycinate 400mg ~45 min before bed
- [x] no screens after 10pm (phone charges in the kitchen, not on the nightstand)
- [x] blackout curtains in the bedroom
- [x] thermostat down to 66°F at 10:30
- [ ] white noise machine vs just a box fan
- [ ] mouth tape?? (saw it on twitter, skeptical)
- [ ] no caffeine after 1pm (currently ~3pm cold brew lol)
- [ ] same wake time on weekends, even sat

## log
**wk 1 — magnesium only**
- felt calmer, hard to say. dozed off maybe 15-20 min faster?
- weird vivid dreams night 3

**wk 2 — + no screens after 10**
- this is the big one. reading a paper book instead (project hail mary reread)
- caught myself reaching for the phone 4x the first two days
- out by ~11:40 most nights. huge

**wk 3 — + blackout curtains**
- the streetlight was def waking me up at 5am before
- woke up groggy twice tho, no morning light. sunrise alarm?

**wk 4 — cooler room**
- 66 feels great once under the duvet, miserable getting out
- Oura says deep stage up ~20 min avg (grain of salt)

## takeaways so far
- screens are the main lever. the rest is like 10%
- magnesium: keep, cheap, no downside
- curtains + sunrise alarm combo → ordered a Hatch Restore
- the 3pm cold brew is probably sabotaging all of this, ugh
