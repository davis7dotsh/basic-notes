# smash burgers

- 80/20 ground beef, 3oz balls, cold
- cast iron/griddle ripping hot, no oil
- smash HARD w/ a stiff spatula + parchment in first 30s, then don't touch
- salt + pepper after smash
- ~2 min, scrape (get all the crust), flip, american cheese immediately, 30s
- potato bun, toasted in the fat
- sauce: mayo, ketchup, pickle relish, dash of mustard, pinch of smoked paprika
- thin sliced onion smashed INTO the patty (oklahoma style) is the move
