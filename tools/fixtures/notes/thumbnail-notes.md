thumbnail notes

learned from the last ~20 uploads + the analytics

- face + 1 object + ≤3 words. every time i break this CTR drops
- high contrast text: white on black stroke, or black on yellow. never thin fonts
- my face looking AT the object, not the camera, does better (+1.2% CTR avg)
- red arrows/circles = cheap, but they work. use sparingly
- test 3 variants w/ youtube's built-in A/B, give it 7 days
- the "I deleted X" framing has the best CTR (8.4%) but watch for misleading-ness
- dark mode bg for code topics, bright for lifestyle-y stuff
- check legibility at 160px wide (mobile home feed)
