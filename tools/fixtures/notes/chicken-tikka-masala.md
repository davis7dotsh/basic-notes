# chicken tikka masala

serves 4. adapted from the serious eats one + kenji's video, less cream

## marinade (overnight ideally, min 2h)
- 2 lb boneless chicken thighs, big chunks
- 1 cup plain whole-milk yogurt
- 1 tbsp garam masala
- 2 tsp kashmiri chili powder
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tbsp grated ginger
- 4 cloves garlic, grated
- 1 tbsp lemon juice, 2 tsp salt

## sauce
- 3 tbsp ghee (or butter)
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 1 tbsp ginger + 4 cloves garlic
- 2 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp coriander, 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 28oz can crushed tomatoes (san marzano)
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 1 tsp kasuri methi (crushed between palms)
- cilantro to finish

## steps
1. skewer chicken, broil on rack ~6 in from element, 10-12 min, flip once. want char spots
2. ghee in dutch oven, onion med heat 10 min till golden
3. ginger/garlic 1 min, spices 30s (bloom), tomato paste 1 min
4. tomatoes, simmer 15 min. blend w/ immersion blender if you want it smooth (I do)
5. cream + methi, then chicken + any juices. simmer 5-10 min
6. salt, squeeze of lemon, cilantro

## notes
- broiler step is what makes it. cast iron screaming hot works too
- the kasuri methi is from the spice shop on 5th, don't skip
- basmati: rinse 3x, 1:1.25 water, 12 min + 10 rest
- leftovers better day 2
- made 10/12 — too salty, cut marinade salt to 1.5 tsp next time
- doubled it for the thanksgiving leftovers idea → freezes fine
