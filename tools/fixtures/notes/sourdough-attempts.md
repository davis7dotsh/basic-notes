# sourdough attempts

starter = "Clint Yeastwood" lol. 100% hydration, fed 1:1:1 w/ AP + 10% whole wheat

## #1 (sep 14)
- 75% hydration, 20% levain, 2% salt
- bulk 4h at ~74°F. underproofed. dense crumb, big tunnel under the crust
- tasted great tho

## #2 (sep 21)
- bulk 6h, jiggly + 50% rise, much better
- shaping was sloppy, spread in the oven
- crumb open-ish, ear barely

## #3 (oct 5)
- dropped to 72% hydration, easier to handle
- 4 sets of coil folds, 30 min apart
- cold retard overnight in fridge 14h
- dutch oven 500°F lid on 20, 450°F lid off 25
- !!! ear, blisters, open crumb. best one so far
