# sheet pan chicken + veg

weeknight default, 40 min, one pan

- 2 lb bone-in chicken thighs, skin on
- 1 lb baby potatoes, halved
- 1 red onion, wedges
- 1 head broccoli
- 4 cloves garlic, smashed
- olive oil, salt, pepper
- 2 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp cumin, 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 lemon

1. oven 425°F. toss potatoes + onion w/ oil, salt, half the spices. spread on pan
2. pat thighs dry (!!), rub w/ rest of spices, skin up on top of the veg
3. 25 min, then add broccoli + garlic around edges, 15 more till skin crisp + 175°F
4. squeeze lemon over everything

- convection 400 works better
- swap broccoli for brussels in the fall
